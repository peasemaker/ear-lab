# Piano sample attribution

Ear Lab uses twenty MP3 samples from **Salamander Grand Piano**, recorded by
**Alexander Holm** on a Yamaha C5 acoustic grand piano.

- Author: Alexander Holm
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Sample source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
- Original project information: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/blob/master/salamander/README

The files C2.mp3, Ds2.mp3, Fs2.mp3, A2.mp3, C3.mp3, Ds3.mp3, Fs3.mp3, A3.mp3,
C4.mp3, Ds4.mp3, Fs4.mp3, A4.mp3,
C5.mp3, Ds5.mp3, Fs5.mp3, A5.mp3, C6.mp3, Ds6.mp3, Fs6.mp3, and A6.mp3
are copied from the MP3 distribution hosted by Tone.js. Ear Lab selects this
subset without editing the audio files. Tone.Sampler transposes the nearest
sample for notes between the recordings and applies playback gain and release.

The piano plays dry, without the ambient background's filtering or reverb.
